VIOT: draw your own incompressible transport

Sketch a source and a target density. The trained operator predicts a divergence-free velocity at every step and transports the source into the target in 50 steps, entirely in your browser with WebGPU.

Paper · Project page · Code · Checkpoints

Examples:

Model: the paper's MNIST operator at 256×256 (FNO backbone, 8 layers), trained only on MNIST digit pairs; its spectral weights are stored in 4 bits (65 MB, see export_web.py). Sketches are recentred, rescaled to fit inside the frame (thin strokes are drawn bolder rather than enlarged past it) and blurred to match the training densities. Serve this folder over HTTP (python -m http.server) and open it in a browser with WebGPU.
